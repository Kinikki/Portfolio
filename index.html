02 / A little about me

Curious by default

Game testing is what pulled me into software. I enjoyed finding unexpected behavior, but I also wanted to know what caused it. That curiosity led me to an associate degree in software development at Sinclair Community College.

Before tech, I managed a tax office, where accuracy, deadlines, and helping people solve problems were part of every day. I bring that same patience to testing and development.

Outside of code, I'm usually gaming, making something, or experimenting with my 3D printer.

What I work with

Java, C#, ASP.NET Core MVC, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, SQL, MySQL, SQL Server, Git, and functional testing.

What I'm looking for

Software development, QA, and testing roles where I can keep learning and contribute to useful work.
