My Background

I'm Mary Brown, a software development graduate with experience in programming, web development, databases, and quality assurance. I enjoy solving problems, figuring out why something isn't working, and turning ideas into functional applications.

My background also includes years of professional experience working with customers, managing responsibilities, and handling detailed information. Those experiences strengthened my communication, organization, troubleshooting, and problem-solving skills, which I now bring into software development and testing.

How I Got Into Tech

My interest in software development grew from working as a remote game tester. During testing sessions, I evaluated game functionality, identified bugs and unexpected behavior, documented issues, and provided feedback on the player experience.

Testing made me curious about what was happening behind the scenes. I wanted to understand how applications were built, why bugs occurred, and how developers solved those problems. That curiosity eventually led me to pursue a degree in software development.

What I've Built

During my software development program, I worked with multiple programming languages, frameworks, and database technologies. My projects included web applications, desktop applications, database-driven systems, object-oriented programs, and applications that processed external data.

I've worked with technologies including Java, C#, C++, ASP.NET Core MVC, SQL, MySQL, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Entity Framework Core, and GitHub.

Through these projects, I gained experience with application design, debugging, database relationships, validation, testing, object-oriented programming, and version control.

What I'm Looking For

I'm looking for an opportunity in software development, quality assurance, software testing, or another entry-level technology role where I can contribute to real projects and continue growing as a developer.

I'm especially interested in work involving troubleshooting, testing, building useful applications, and improving the experience of the people using them.

Beyond the Code

Outside of development, I enjoy gaming, creative projects, 3D printing, and spending time with my family. I especially enjoy projects where creativity and technology overlap, whether I'm building an application, testing a game, or designing something to print.
